# Nothing happens without a record you can audit
# Source: https://n8n.io/enterprise/

See every workflow run, every change, and every action, with the records you need to investigate and audit.

## See how n8n is used across the business
Insights shows executions, failure rates, and time saved for every workflow, agent, and project.

## Full audit trail, tied to identity
See who created, edited, or deleted a workflow and when. Every change is tied to a user.

## Step-by-step execution history
Open any past run to see each step's inputs, outputs, and failures. For agents, see which tools they used and when a human stepped in.

## Logs and metrics in your stack
Stream audit events, logs, and metrics to Splunk, Datadog, Grafana, or your OpenTelemetry endpoint.
